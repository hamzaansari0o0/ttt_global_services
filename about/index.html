About TTT Global Services

A trusted partner connecting Pakistan and China through education, trade, and travel since 2020.

Get in Touch

Who We Are

TTT Global Services was founded with a singular vision: to create meaningful connections between Pakistan and China. Operating at the intersection of education, commerce, and travel, we bring together the best of both nations to serve students, business professionals, and travelers.

With a dedicated team of language instructors, trade consultants, and travel specialists, we deliver end-to-end solutions that are professional, culturally sensitive, and results-oriented. Our name — Training, Trading, Traveling — reflects our three core pillars that define everything we do.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and businesses by providing world-class Chinese language education, reliable China–Pakistan trade facilitation, and seamless travel services — all under one roof.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted bridge between Pakistan and China — enabling cultural exchange, economic partnerships, and educational growth for generations to come.

Our Core Values

Integrity

Honest, ethical, and transparent in every interaction.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest standard of service.

Cultural Bridge

Fostering understanding between Pakistan and China.

Partnership

Building long-term relationships rooted in mutual trust.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the growth of TTT Global Services.

2020

Company Founded

TTT Global Services was established in Lahore, Pakistan with a vision to bridge Pakistan and China.

2021

Language Department Launch

Launched Chinese language training programs with qualified native and bilingual instructors.

2022

Trading Services Expansion

Expanded into China–Pakistan import/export trading with verified supplier networks.

2023

Travel Division Established

Added comprehensive travel services including visa assistance, study tours, and tourism packages.

2024

Scholarship Partnerships

Partnered with Chinese universities to support CSC and institutional scholarship applicants.

2026

Growing Stronger

Continuing to expand services and build new partnerships across both nations.
