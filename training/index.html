FAQs

Chinese Language Classes FAQs

Where can I learn Chinese in Lahore?

TTT Global Services offers Chinese language classes in Lahore for learners at beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels. Contact us to discuss the right class or schedule for you.

Do you offer beginner Chinese classes?

Yes. Our Beginner Chinese course covers Pinyin, essential characters, greetings, daily phrases, and basic conversation through HSK 1–2-level learning.

Do you provide HSK preparation?

Yes. We provide structured HSK preparation for Levels 1–6, with progression in vocabulary, reading, listening, speaking, and exam-focused practice.

Do you offer online Chinese classes?

Yes. Online Chinese classes are available for learners in Pakistan who need a flexible way to study with guided instruction and digital learning materials.

Do you offer Business Chinese?

Yes. Our Business Chinese training covers trade terminology, professional communication, negotiation scripts, and email and contract language.

How long do the Chinese language courses take?

Beginner Chinese typically takes three months, intermediate Chinese six months, and advanced Chinese twelve months. Business Chinese and personalized tutoring can be arranged on a flexible schedule.
